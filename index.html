Phở ir mājas specialitātePhở is the house speciality

Dzidrs, saldens buljons, kas vairākas stundas vārīts uz kauliem, rīsu nūdeles, liellopu gaļa vai vista, sīpoli, ingvers un svaigi garšaugi. Tā phở apraksta pati SAPA un piebilst, ka būt Rīgā un to nenogaršot tiešām būtu žēl.A clear, slightly sweet broth simmered on bones for several hours, rice noodles, beef or chicken, onion, ginger and fresh herbs. That is how SAPA describes its own phở, adding that being in Rīga without trying it really would be a pity.
